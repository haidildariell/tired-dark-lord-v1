## The monologue

Sable the Warlock, Master of the Ninth Circle, receives a hero who came to
bargain — and finds his master mid-negotiation with a cat. Mister Whiskers
has filed a formal grievance, in triplicate. The apocalypse is on hold until
the cushion situation is resolved.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the monologue (98 words) to `/output/entry.txt`.
